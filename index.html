About Me

Hello! I am Aman Shah, a passionate software developer with a strong focus on building accessible, semantic, and user-centered web applications. With several years of experience in front-end and full-stack development, I enjoy turning complex problems into clean, maintainable solutions. My journey began with a curiosity for how the web works, and it has evolved into a career dedicated to creating meaningful digital experiences.

When I am not coding, I explore open-source contributions, write technical articles, and experiment with emerging web standards. I believe in continuous learning and the power of semantic HTML to improve both accessibility and search engine visibility.
